## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

**The short version:** I don't sell or share your personal information, and this site doesn't run ads.

**Site statistics.** This site counts page views with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics service. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect personal information, and doesn't track you across other websites. It records anonymous totals, such as which pages are viewed, the referring site, and browser and screen size. This site does not use Google Analytics or any advertising or tracking cookies.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.

**Email.** The only way to contact me through this site is by email. If you write to me, I'll use your address only to reply, and I won't add you to any list without asking.

**Embedded content and links.** Some pages embed or link to other sites, such as YouTube, Notion and Google Drive. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.

**Questions.** Email [dionysiusbuhler@gmail.com](mailto:dionysiusbuhler@gmail.com).
